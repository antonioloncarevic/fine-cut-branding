Brand Breakdown

Bright grounds, sharp accents.

Fine Cut is light-first. White carries most layouts, Electric Blue pulls the eye, Dark Blue anchors statements, and green and red only ever mean right and wrong.

Usage ratio across a deck

62 / 15 / 10 / 8 / 3 / 2. White grounds dominate, Electric marks sections and CTAs, Dark Blue takes statements and quotes, Ink carries text, and green and red stay small so they keep their meaning. Soft Red #F08A8A and Soft Green #96D791 are full-slide grounds for wrong-and-right reveals only.

Brand Breakdown

The rules that keep it consistent.

Do

  • Start from White. Switch to Electric or Dark Blue only to mark a section or a statement.
  • Put the "Fine Cut" wordmark top-left and "finecut.io" bottom-right on every slide.
  • Use one italic accent per headline, one to three words, in one flat color.
  • Keep green for wins and right answers, and red for mistakes and wrong answers.
  • Build lists one bullet per slide when you present live.

Don't

  • Use green or red as decoration. They always carry meaning.
  • Use Beige as a background, anywhere.
  • Set Electric text on Dark Blue. It disappears.
  • Use Playfair for anything except the wordmark.
  • Add blurred glow blobs behind content, or let any text clip.
